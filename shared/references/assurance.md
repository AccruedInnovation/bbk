# Proportional Assurance

Use four non-averaging axes: consequence, irreversibility, uncertainty, and interface exposure.

| Tier | Default evidence | Independent review | Suite policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Routine | focused deterministic checks | not required by default | focused |
| Material | affected checks and applicable adverse/consumer path | one assertion-scoped validator | affected |
| Consequential | focused + applicable consumer/fault/integration evidence | one independent acceptance review | full once after freeze |
| Critical | defense in depth against protected floor or feared event | complementary independent methods and accountable authority | profile mandate |

Prove each material assertion once by the cheapest sufficient method. Reject accidental reviewer overlap. Reuse evidence only when its complete dependency fingerprint is unchanged. `PASS`, `FAIL`, `BLOCKED`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, and `SKIPPED` are distinct.
