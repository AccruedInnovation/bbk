# Review Independence

Record author separation, role separation, invocation/session separation, independent context assembly, prior-finding visibility, model/provider diversity, deterministic-evidence independence, organizational independence, and subject-mutation prohibition separately. No single dimension proves sufficient independence for every assertion. Use `HIDDEN` for blind reassessment, `TARGETED` for closure, `FULL` for synthesis/adjudication, and `NOT_APPLICABLE` for deterministic or first-run work.

Also record logical-role-to-physical-invocation mapping and append-only outcome-evidence exposure. A fresh session is not independent when it receives criteria or summaries selected after the same outcome evidence was observed. Label exploratory, replication, robustness, targeted-closure, adjudication, and confirmatory attempts accurately.
