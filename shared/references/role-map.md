# Role Map

The visible harness root controller is the sole user-facing identity. It selects and supervises one canonical BBK root role but does not impersonate Wayfinding, orchestration, implementation, review, or validation. Every canonical BBK role is a non-user-facing child. Human decisions, authority requests, private context, protected-floor exceptions, and explicit acceptance travel through the host-native child-to-root channel; in OMP this is `hub`/IRC to the peer whose kind is `main`.

Planning roles own outcomes, boundaries, questions, architecture, work design, and assurance. Root and Territory Wayfinders delegate clusters of human-decision work through a logical Questioning Wayfinder, which prepares exact Question Guide context, manages branch continuity, and returns ADR-compatible packets. Execution orchestrators own sequencing, containment, workspace coordination, and escalation but not planning semantics. Workers mutate only their assigned scope. Validators inspect exact assertions against exact candidates and never repair or waive findings. Logical roles are distinct from physical invocations: co-location is allowed only when it preserves authority, result, interaction, and independence boundaries.
