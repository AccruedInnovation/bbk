# Procedure Design

Keep performer definition, reusable organizational procedure, and one authorized execution baseline distinct. Procedures may be deterministic, bounded-adaptive, plan-proposing, exploratory, or interactive-decision. Bind entry conditions, steps/transitions, logical roles, context edges, result envelopes, authority checks, effects, gates, evidence, stopping, recovery, and completion. Preserve findings, repair, approval, and commit as separate operations. Procedures are immutable method records; they cannot authorize or activate themselves.
